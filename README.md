## Product

Like online dating, listings show their best side. We help you check what’s behind the listing before you do.

Buying a home is emotional. You’re scrolling, imagining your life there, picturing the layout, the light, the neighborhood. But most buyers are evaluating what’s visible on the surface, while the real risks are buried in details that take time, effort, and experience to uncover.

TrueListRE helps you cut through that. As you browse on Redfin or Zillow, it automatically cross-checks listing data against county records, ownership history, and cross-site estimates to surface the signals you’d otherwise have to dig for yourself. Discrepancies in square footage, year built, or lot size, unusual valuation gaps, and other red flags are highlighted instantly so you know what deserves a second look.

About 1 in 10 home sales fall through before closing, often after issues are discovered too late. TrueListRE is designed to shift that discovery earlier. Instead of spending hours piecing together information across tabs or learning things during inspection, you get a clear score and key findings up front. It’s a fast, practical layer of due diligence that helps you move forward with confidence, not surprises.
